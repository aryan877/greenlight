Privacy

Updated August 30, 2026

What Greenlight stores

Greenlight stores the account needed to enter the studio, project media, editing history, generated artifacts, approval history, and the YouTube authorization you choose to connect.

How it is used

This data is used only to run your production workspace, preserve your edits, render your video, and perform the YouTube actions you approve. Greenlight does not sell personal data.

YouTube access

Greenlight requests permission to upload and manage videos on the channel you connect. Uploads are staged for review first. Public or scheduled release requires a separate approval for the exact video snapshot.

Control

You can disconnect Greenlight from your Google Account permissions at any time. Contact aryankumar877@gmail.com to request deletion of demo account or project data.
